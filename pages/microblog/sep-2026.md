## 01 SEP 2026 Tue - JP Bouvet

I'm really digging JP's drumming course. I'm on to the "Triplet Vocabulary" course and can totally see how this will lead to endless flow, and I'm loving the triplet feel. Not even doing swing yet, but still... I've made [my own loops of building blocks](https://ashleyf.github.io/wiki/#/music/triplet-vocabulary). ❤️

## 02 SEP 2026 Wed - Calvin

I went to lunch with Calvin this afternoon. We had a bunch of fun conversations with hypothetical situations. Like, "Do you think a Minority Report kind of thing, with precogs and arrests for crimes not commited yet, is a good thing?" I think it depends on first beliving in determinism and then it's okay. And the arrests are less about punishment, because you were destined to do it, and more about simply preventing the crime. Another question was around eugenics. Anyway, he was coming up with the questions and it was fun.

Once I got home, I drummed for a couple of hours. Did the Drumeo method and practiced some things from the JP Bouvet class I'm on. I'm really liking the JP stuff. I think it's much more "coherent" than Drumeo and structured much more like a weekly (online) drum instructor; with a lessons introducing a lot, but then a week's worth of "homework" to do on your own. And I like the idea of assigned mentors and progress videos.
