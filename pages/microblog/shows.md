# Shows

## Queen's Gambit (31 JUL 2026 - 02 AUG 2026)

We watched this when it first came out. Excellent show. Tracey's been getting into Chess lately, so this is fun to revisit.

## Untold: Chess Mates (08 AUG 2026)

This is a documentary on the Hans Niemann controversy in the chess world. Magnus Carlson and others accused him of cheating in across-the-board games. He had admitted to cheating on chess.com. It was pretty interesting and presented as a real mystery unravelling.

## Magnus Carlson on Joe Rogan (07 AUG 2026 - 08 AUG 2026)

This was a very interesting interview, for someone who likes chess. It definitely felt like Joe didn't know how to interview him. He answers questions very directly, and so it would take a good interviewer to keep him going. Joe was our of his element. He had another guy there who was a chess-nut friend of Joe's. That helped a bit. Anyway though, they talked for hours and covered all kinds of interesting things and lots of little nuggets.
