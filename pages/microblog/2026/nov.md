# NOV 2026

## 01 NOV 2026 Sun - Toys

I spent the day playing with the MPK mini IV, trying all the software that comes with it and controlling it myself. By reverse engineering their website, we (Grok and I) could tell that it takes SysEx commands to control the LED colors behind the pad, reset the infinite knobs, and display ASCII text on the screen. I made a little demo to cycle rainbow colors and to do a "KITT car"-style light bar.

One annoying thing is that MIDI for the keybed and the pad is all sent on the same channel, so I can't easily map the pads to Superior Drummer and the keys to EZKeys. So I made a little router. I added a pair of "Drums" and "Keys" buses in MIDI Studio (nice feature on the Mac) and routed appropriately, then configured SD and EZKeys to listen to each bus. Works! I can simultaneously use piano keys and SD drums and can map things like the mod wheel to control the hi-hat pedal.

Then I had the idea of taking [Vincent Riemer's TR-808 emulator](http://io808.com) and controlling it from the MPK. Grok and I pulled this off in about an hour and I spent the rest of the night playing with it.
