MIDI setup

Enable MIDI, choose the virtual or physical port received by Superior Drummer, and use the same channel there. Send MIDI clock transmits tempo at the standard 24 pulses per quarter note, plus Start and Stop messages. Notes use the General MIDI drum map on channel 10 by default. Hi-hat hits also send pedal CC4 (127 closed, 0 open) so Superior Drummer's e-drum mapping receives an explicit openness state. Browser volume and mute affect only the built-in sounds; MIDI continues playing.

Superior Drummer libraries can assign additional aliases or substitute articulations. Its Help → MIDI Mapping Layout shows the definitive map for the loaded library.
